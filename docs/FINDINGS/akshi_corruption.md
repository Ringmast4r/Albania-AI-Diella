# AKSHI Corruption Scandal (December 2025)
## The Irony of Albania's Anti-Corruption AI

---

## Summary

In December 2025, just three months after Diella was appointed as Albania's AI Minister to "fight corruption," the leadership of AKSHI (the agency that built Diella) was arrested on corruption charges.

---

## Key Arrests

### Mirlinda Karçanaj - Director General of AKSHI
- **Status:** Under house arrest
- **Date:** December 2025
- **Charges:**
  - Participation in a structured criminal group
  - Tender manipulation
  - Abuse of office

### Deputy Director
- **Status:** Under house arrest
- **Date:** December 2025
- **Charges:** Same as above

---

## Investigation Details

### Scope
- **12 tender procedures** under investigation
- Multiple years of alleged manipulation
- Systematic abuse of public procurement

### Investigating Bodies
- SPAK (Special Anti-Corruption Structure)
- Albanian Prosecution

### BIRN Report Finding
> "Capture of AKSHI by criminal interests endangers national security"

## Implications

1. **Credibility Crisis:** The agency responsible for Albania's digital transformation and anti-corruption AI was itself engaged in corruption

2. **National Security:** BIRN report suggests the capture of AKSHI by criminal interests poses security risks to Albanian government systems

3. **Data Security:** Given AKSHI's control over:
   - e-Albania (citizen data for 600+ services)
   - GOVnet (220 government institutions)
   - Diella (access to 36,000+ documents)
   - The question arises: what data may have been compromised?

4. **EU Accession:** This scandal damages Albania's case for EU membership, which requires demonstrated anti-corruption progress
